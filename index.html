About Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC is a family-owned and operated plumbing company based in Goodyear, Arizona. With more than 25 years of hands-on experience, we provide dependable residential and light-commercial plumbing services throughout Goodyear and the West Valley. Our team handles everything from everyday plumbing repairs, drain cleaning, water-heater replacement, fixture repairs, and sewer issues to slab leaks, water-line replacement, whole-home repiping, gas-line installation, remodel plumbing, and new-construction plumbing. We are committed to honest communication, quality workmanship, and practical solutions that help customers protect their homes and businesses. Arizona ROC #345448.

Published resources cover slab leak detection and repair, whole-home repiping and water-line replacement, drain cleaning and camera inspections, sewer-line diagnosis and repair, water-heater replacement and installation, natural gas-line installation, residential and light-commercial plumbing, free plumbing estimates, single line water re-pipes, complete water re-pipes, and 190 additional service areas.

Service coverage includes Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, Waddel, Sun City, Sun City West , Tonapha, Tolleson, Peoria and 5 more communities.
