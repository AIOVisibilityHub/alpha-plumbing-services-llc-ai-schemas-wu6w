Give the sensor a gentle wipe-down and make sure the area around it is clear. However, if those easy fixes don't work, you might be dealing with something a bit more involved, like a faulty solenoid valve (which controls the water flow) or a problem with the wiring itself.

These systems are more complex than traditional faucets, combining electronics with plumbing. Trying to diagnose or repair a deeper electrical or valve issue yourself can sometimes cause more problems than it solves, or even damage the unit further.
