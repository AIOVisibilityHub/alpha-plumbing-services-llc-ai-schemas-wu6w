Mistakes to Avoid When Your Main Line is Backed Up

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A main line backup is one of those plumbing nightmares no homeowner wants to face, but knowing what not to do can save you a lot of grief and money. One common mistake is assuming it's just a small clog and repeatedly using harsh chemical drain cleaners. These chemicals are rarely effective for main line blockages and can actually corrode your pipes over time, making future repairs more complicated.

Another pitfall is trying to use a small household snake or auger for a main line problem. These tools typically aren't long enough or powerful enough to clear deeper, more stubborn blockages, and you could end up pushing the problem further down the line or even damaging your pipes. Ignoring persistent slow drains or gurgling sounds is also a big mistake; these are early warnings that can escalate into a full-blown backup if left unaddressed.

And perhaps the biggest mistake? Delaying calling a professional. A main line backup is a serious issue that usually requires specialized equipment and expertise to diagnose and resolve safely and effectively.
