When to Call for Hidden Water Leak Detection

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Hidden water leaks are truly sneaky. They can be causing damage behind your walls or under your floors for ages before you even notice them, which is why knowing when to call for professional leak detection is so crucial. Catching these problems early can literally save you thousands in repairs and prevent serious damage to your home’s structure and your family’s health from mold growth. It’s all about spotting those subtle signs.

Keep an eye out for several key indicators. Has your water bill suddenly jumped up without any change in your usage habits? That's a classic sign that water is escaping somewhere it shouldn't be.

Another big one is hearing the sound of running water even when all your fixtures are off—this can point to a leak within your pipes. You might also notice unexplained wet spots on your floors or ceilings, or discoloration on your walls. Don't ignore musty odors; mold thrives in damp, hidden areas and often gives off a distinct smell.

Finally, if you're seeing persistent low water pressure throughout your home, it could be due to a leak reducing the flow. These symptoms are your home's way of telling you there's a problem brewing.
