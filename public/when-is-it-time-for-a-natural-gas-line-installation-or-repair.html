When Is It Time for a Natural Gas Line Installation or Repair?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Knowing when to consider a new natural gas line installation or a repair on an existing one is pretty important for your home's safety and functionality. The most urgent sign, of course, is that distinct rotten egg smell – if you detect that, it's an immediate emergency and you should follow safety protocols right away by evacuating and calling 911 and your gas company. Beyond emergencies, you might need a new installation if you're looking to add a new gas appliance, like a stove, dryer, or outdoor grill, and don't have an existing hookup.

Or maybe you're renovating and want to relocate an appliance, which often requires extending or rerouting a gas line. For repairs, look out for frequently extinguished pilot lights on your gas appliances or if an appliance isn't performing as efficiently as it should, which could indicate a minor leak or pressure issue. Visible damage to an outdoor gas line, or changes in vegetation around buried lines, are also signals for professional inspection.

Gas line work isn't a DIY job; it requires specialized knowledge and adherence to strict safety codes.
