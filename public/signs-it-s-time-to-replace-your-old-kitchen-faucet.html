Signs It's Time to Replace Your Old Kitchen Faucet

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Your kitchen faucet works hard every day, so it’s natural for it to show signs of wear and tear over time. But how do you know when it’s truly time to replace it rather than just trying another repair? Recognizing the red flags can save you money, prevent water damage, and generally make your kitchen life a lot smoother. You don't want to wait until it completely gives out on you!

One of the most obvious signs is persistent leaking or dripping. Even after replacing washers or cartridges, if your faucet keeps dripping, it might be signaling its last days. That constant drip not only wastes water but can also drive up your water bill.

Another big clue is visible rust or corrosion, especially around the base or handles. This isn't just an aesthetic problem; it can indicate that the internal components are deteriorating, making future leaks more likely and potentially affecting water quality.

If you’re experiencing low water pressure specifically from your kitchen faucet, and not elsewhere in the house, it could mean mineral buildup inside the faucet itself. While sometimes cleanable, if it's severe, a replacement might be more effective.

Difficult operation, like handles that are stiff, squeaky, or hard to turn, also points to internal wear. You shouldn't have to wrestle with your faucet just to get water.

Finally, if you're frequently calling for repairs, or the cost of repairs is starting to add up, it's often more economical in the long run to invest in a brand-new, reliable faucet.
