My new gas appliance needs a connection, what do I do?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

So, you've got a new gas appliance, maybe a fancy range or a cozy fireplace, and now you're wondering about the hook-up. It's exciting, but getting that gas line installed correctly is super important for safety and efficiency. This isn't really a DIY job, as there are very specific codes and safety protocols to follow.

First off, you'll need to figure out if your existing gas line can handle the additional demand of the new appliance. Sometimes, the current setup just isn't designed for extra capacity, and trying to push it can lead to low pressure or even hazardous conditions. You'll also need to consider the distance from the main line to the appliance and what kind of piping is best for that run, whether it's flexible or rigid.

Improper connections or materials can cause leaks, which are seriously dangerous. It’s always best to have a professional check your current system, plan the new line, and handle the installation. They'll ensure everything is up to code and, more importantly, safe for your family.
