When to Call About a Leaky Tub

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Noticing a drip, drip, drip from your tub? It might seem minor, but ignoring a leaky tub can lead to bigger, more expensive problems down the road, like water damage to your floors or even structural issues. You definitely don't want that!

If you're seeing persistent puddles around the base of your tub, discolored or soft spots on your ceiling below (if you have an upstairs bathroom), or even a noticeable increase in your water bill, it's a pretty strong sign that something's amiss. Sometimes, it’s just a worn-out faucet washer, which is a fairly straightforward fix. Other times, it could be a more complex issue with the drain assembly or even a crack in the tub itself.
