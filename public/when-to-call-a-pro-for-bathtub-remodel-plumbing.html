When to Call a Pro for Bathtub Remodel Plumbing

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about a bathtub remodel? It’s an exciting project, but the plumbing behind it can be trickier than it looks. While some DIY enthusiasts might tackle minor cosmetic updates, knowing when to bring in a professional plumber like us at Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC can save you a lot of hassle and expense down the road. We've got over 25 years of hands-on experience, so we've seen it all!

One big sign it's time to call us is if you're planning to move your bathtub's location significantly. This isn't just about disconnecting and reconnecting; it often involves rerouting water supply lines and drain pipes, which requires expertise in ensuring proper drainage and avoiding leaks. Another key indicator is if your home is older and its existing plumbing system might not be up to code or can't handle the demands of a new, larger tub or a different style of faucet. We can assess your current setup and recommend the best, most practical solutions.

If you encounter unexpected issues like rotting wood, mold, or damaged pipes once you start demo, that's definitely a call-a-pro moment. These problems need immediate attention from someone who knows how to fix them safely and effectively before your new tub goes in. Also, installing high-end or complex fixtures, such as body sprays or jetted tubs, usually requires specialized knowledge to ensure they function perfectly and meet manufacturer specifications. Don't risk damaging expensive new components by attempting a DIY job.

Finally, if you just want peace of mind, that’s a perfectly valid reason to hire us. We pride ourselves on quality workmanship and honest communication, ensuring your remodel plumbing is done right the first time.
