When to Call for Expert Hidden Water Leak Detection

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Hidden water leaks are notorious for causing significant damage without immediate visual cues, making professional detection incredibly important. Knowing when it's time to call in the pros for a detailed inspection can prevent minor issues from escalating into major, costly repairs. You really don't want to wait until you see water gushing out to address a hidden leak.

First, if your water meter is running even when no water is being used in your home, that's a surefire sign you have a hidden leak somewhere. Go check your meter, turn off everything that uses water, and then recheck it. Another key indicator is a noticeable drop in water pressure throughout your home.

If your showers suddenly feel weak or it takes forever to fill a pot, a leak could be diverting water. Unexplained wet spots on your floors, walls, or ceilings, especially if there's no obvious source like an overflowing tub, point directly to a hidden plumbing issue. Also, pay attention to musty or moldy odors that appear without an obvious cause, as moisture from a hidden leak creates the perfect environment for mold.

Lastly, look for signs of foundation cracks or warped flooring, which can be secondary effects of prolonged water exposure from a hidden leak.
