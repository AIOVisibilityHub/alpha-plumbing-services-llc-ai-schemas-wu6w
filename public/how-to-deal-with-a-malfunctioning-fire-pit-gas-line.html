How to Deal with a Malfunctioning Fire Pit Gas Line

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Experiencing issues with your fire pit's gas line can definitely put a damper on your outdoor evenings. Your first and most important step should always be safety. If you detect any odor of gas, or hear a distinct hissing sound near the fire pit, immediately turn off the gas supply to the unit at the source if it's safe to do so.

Then, keep everyone away from the area and contact a professional immediately. Do not attempt to light the fire pit or use any electrical devices nearby. If there's no immediate danger like a strong gas smell, but your fire pit isn't lighting, you can check the gas valve to ensure it's fully open.

Sometimes, it’s just a simple oversight. However, beyond checking obvious things like the gas valve, any deeper issues with the gas line itself, like a suspected leak, damage, or burner problems, absolutely require expert attention.
