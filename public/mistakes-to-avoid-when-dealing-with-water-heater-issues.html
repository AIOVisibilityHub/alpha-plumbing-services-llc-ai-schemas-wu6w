Mistakes to Avoid When Dealing With Water Heater Issues

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When your water heater is on the fritz, it's easy to want to jump in and try to fix it yourself, but there are some common blunders folks make that can actually worsen the situation. First, don't ignore strange noises or discolored water; hoping it goes away rarely works and often leads to bigger repairs. Second, trying to force a leaky valve or connection can strip threads or cause a bigger leak—gentle is better, or just leave it.

Third, don't attempt to drain a hot water heater without turning off the power and water supply first; this is a safety hazard and could damage the heating elements. Fourth, installing a new water heater yourself without proper permits or experience can lead to safety risks and even code violations, which means trouble later. Fifth, buying the cheapest replacement unit might save money upfront, but it often costs more in efficiency and longevity.

Always prioritize quality and proper installation. Lastly, don't forget about regular maintenance; a little check-up can prevent a lot of headaches. For any serious water heater issues or installations, it’s always wise to reach out to experienced professionals like Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC.
