Is It Time to Replace My Old Faucet?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Every faucet has its lifespan, and knowing when to swap out an old one for something new can save you a lot of grief (and potentially money!) down the road. It’s not always about a catastrophic failure; sometimes, replacement just makes more sense than endless repairs.

Here are some clear signs that it might be time to say goodbye to your old faucet and welcome a shiny new one:

  • Frequent Leaks and Repairs: If your faucet is constantly dripping or requiring repairs for various issues – O-rings, washers, cartridges – it’s probably reached the end of its reliable life. Continuously patching up an old faucet can become more expensive than installing a new one.
  • Visible Rust, Corrosion, or Damage: Beyond just looking bad, rust and corrosion can compromise the faucet's integrity, leading to leaks you can't easily fix. Pitting, cracks, or heavy mineral buildup that won't clean off are also good indicators.
  • Outdated Style or Poor Water Pressure: While aesthetic isn't a functional issue, if your faucet looks like it's from another era and you're tired of it, that's a good reason! Also, if you're experiencing consistently low water pressure that isn't a whole-house issue, your old faucet might be the culprit due to internal blockages.
  • Renovating Your Kitchen or Bathroom: If you’re already upgrading your sink, countertops, or entire space, it’s the perfect opportunity to install a new, matching faucet. It integrates seamlessly with your new design and avoids having an old faucet detract from your fresh look.

Replacing a faucet isn’t always a simple DIY task, especially with new styles or if your existing plumbing needs adjustments.
