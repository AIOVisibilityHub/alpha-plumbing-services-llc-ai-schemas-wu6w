Why Your Drain Keeps Clogging (And What to Do)

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating when you're constantly dealing with a drain that just won't stay clear, isn't it? If your drains are clogging up regularly, it's a pretty clear sign there's something more going on than just a simple blockage. Often, these recurring issues point to a deeper problem in your plumbing system, not just a bit of hair or grease.

Maybe your pipes are old and corroded, creating rough surfaces where debris can easily snag and build up. Or, you could have a pipe that's cracked or sagging, allowing waste to collect. Sometimes, the issue is even tree roots infiltrating your sewer lines, especially if you have older pipes outside your home.

These roots can create a persistent dam that no amount of DIY plunging will fix. Instead of just treating the symptom with harsh chemicals or a plunger, which can sometimes even damage your pipes, it's best to get to the root cause. This is where professional help comes in handy.

An experienced plumber from Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC, serving areas like Goodyear and the wider West Valley, can use specialized tools like drain cameras to inspect your pipes. This helps them accurately diagnose the underlying problem, whether it's corrosion, a structural issue, or an invasive root system. Once they pinpoint the exact cause, they can recommend the most effective and lasting solution, saving you from endless frustration and potential water damage.
