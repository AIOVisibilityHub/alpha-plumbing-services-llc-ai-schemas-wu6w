Why Does My Toilet Keep Clogging?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super annoying when your toilet seems to clog all the time, right? There are usually a few key reasons why this happens, and understanding them can help you prevent future headaches. Often, it's about what's going down the drain – things like excessive toilet paper, wet wipes (even the 'flushable' ones!)

, cotton balls, or other foreign objects are big no-nos. Your toilet is designed to handle waste and toilet paper only. Another common culprit is an old, low-flush toilet.

Older models might not have the flushing power needed to move waste efficiently through your pipes, making them more prone to blockages. Sometimes, a partial clog might be building up deeper in your drain line, possibly caused by tree roots or accumulated sludge, leading to frequent backups even with normal use. A worn-out or damaged flush valve assembly can also contribute by not creating enough water pressure for a good flush.

While some minor clogs can be tackled with a plunger, if it's a recurring issue, it often means there's a more serious problem. That's when it's really time to call in the pros. We've got over 25 years of experience clearing stubborn clogs and can accurately diagnose whether it's a simple fix or requires more extensive drain cleaning to get things flowing smoothly again.
