What to do when you suspect an underground water leak?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Suspecting an underground water leak can be unsettling, but knowing the right steps can prevent further damage and stress. First off, don't panic! Start by checking your water meter.

If it's constantly spinning even when all water fixtures are off in your home, you likely have a leak somewhere. Next, observe your outdoor areas. Look for unusually green patches of grass, soft or soggy spots in your yard, or areas where the pavement looks discolored or sunken.

These are all common indicators. Don't try to dig around blindly; you could hit other utility lines or make the problem worse. Avoid delaying action, as even a small leak can lead to significant water waste, foundation damage, or even attract pests.

It's also a mistake to assume it will fix itself. Underground leaks almost always require professional intervention. The best course of action is to contact an experienced plumbing service, like Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC.
