Is Your Tub Beyond a Simple Fix?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It’s a common question we get: 'Can my tub be fixed, or do I need a whole new one?' It really depends on the issue, but there are definitely signs that point towards needing more than just a quick repair. If you're seeing deep cracks or significant chips in the enamel, especially if they're reaching the sub-surface, a simple patch might not cut it for long-term durability or aesthetics.

Major structural damage, like a sagging tub floor, definitely means it’s time to consider a replacement. Also, if you're constantly dealing with recurring leaks, mold issues that won't go away, or a general dingy appearance that no amount of cleaning can fix, replacing the tub might be the most practical and cost-effective solution in the long run. We also see tubs with outdated designs that just don't fit modern bathrooms, making replacement a great option for a full bathroom refresh.
