When to Call: Gurgling Drains Becoming a Concern

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's no fun hearing those strange gurgling sounds coming from your sinks, tubs, or toilets. Often, it's an early warning sign that something's not quite right with your home's plumbing system. While it might seem minor at first, persistent gurgling can point to a variety of issues, some of which require professional attention to prevent bigger problems. Knowing when to reach out to the experts can save you from potential water damage and expensive repairs down the line.

If the gurgling noises happen every time you use a specific fixture, or even when you use a different fixture nearby, that's a key indicator. For instance, if flushing a toilet causes your shower drain to gurgle, you likely have a shared drain line that's partially obstructed. Another clear signal to call in a professional is if the gurgling is accompanied by very slow draining, or if you start seeing standing water in your sinks or tubs.

These combined symptoms almost always mean there's a significant clog building up somewhere in your plumbing system. If you've tried simple fixes like plungers and the gurgling continues, or if it seems to be getting worse, it's definitely time to bring in the pros. Also, any unpleasant odors emanating from the drains along with the gurgling point to a buildup of waste that needs to be addressed promptly.
