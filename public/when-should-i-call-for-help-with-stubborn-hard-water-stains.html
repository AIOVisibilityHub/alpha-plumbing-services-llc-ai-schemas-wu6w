When Should I Call for Help with Stubborn Hard Water Stains?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Hard water stains can be a real nuisance, making your bathrooms and kitchens look less than sparkling. While a little elbow grease and some household cleaners can often tackle minor buildup, there are definite signs it’s time to call in a professional. Ignoring these signals can lead to more significant plumbing issues down the line.

If you're noticing significant mineral buildup on your fixtures that doesn't budge with regular cleaning, that's a key indicator. We're talking about thick, crusty deposits that even strong cleaners can't penetrate. Another sign is when your water pressure starts to noticeably drop from faucets or showerheads; these mineral deposits can actually constrict pipes and aerators.

You might also observe that your soap isn't lathering as well as it used to, or your dishes are coming out of the dishwasher with spots, even after using rinse aid. Repeated repairs of small leaks or frequently replacing fixture parts due to corrosion are also red flags.
