Is That Smell Really a Gas Leak?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

That distinct 'rotten egg' smell is usually the tell-tale sign of a natural gas leak, and it's something you should never ignore. Gas companies actually add a chemical called mercaptan to natural gas precisely because it’s odorless on its own, making leaks easy to detect. So, if you're smelling something like that in your home or around your property, even faintly, it’s crucial to act fast.

Don't assume it's just something outside or a passing whiff; your nose is your first and best detector here. Beyond the smell, sometimes you might hear a faint whistling or hissing sound near a gas line or appliance, which could also indicate a leak. Plants unexpectedly dying near outdoor gas lines can also be a subtle sign.
