Mistakes to Avoid When You Suspect a Main Sewer Line Clog

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you suspect a main sewer line clog, it’s easy to panic, but some common mistakes can actually make the situation worse. First, don't keep flushing toilets or running water if you see backups. This just adds more water to the system, increasing the risk of an overflow in your home.

Immediately stop using water-reliant appliances like washing machines and dishwashers too. Second, avoid pouring harsh chemical drain cleaners down the drain. These products are rarely effective against main sewer line clogs, can corrode your pipes, and can create dangerous fumes or burns for anyone who has to work on the line later.

Third, don't try to clear a main sewer line clog with a small, handheld auger. These clogs are usually deep and require professional-grade equipment and expertise to clear them safely and completely. You could push the blockage further or even damage your pipes.

Fourth, don't put off calling a professional. The longer you wait, the more severe the damage could become, potentially leading to costly repairs.
