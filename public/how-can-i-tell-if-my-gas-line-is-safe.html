How can I tell if my gas line is safe?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Wondering if your natural gas line is in good shape and safe for your home? It's a smart question, and there are a few signs you can look out for that might signal an issue. One of the most obvious red flags is the smell of rotten eggs, which is an additive put into natural gas to make leaks detectable.

If you smell that, it's critical to act immediately: leave the building, call your gas company, and then a professional plumber. Another sign could be a hissing sound near a gas appliance or meter, which indicates a potential leak. You might also notice a drop in appliance performance, like a gas stove burner that isn't lighting properly, or an unexplained increase in your gas bill, which could point to an inefficient or leaking line.

Lastly, if you have older gas pipes, especially if they show signs of corrosion or rust, it's definitely time for an inspection. Even small changes can sometimes indicate a bigger problem. Don't try to fix these issues yourself!
