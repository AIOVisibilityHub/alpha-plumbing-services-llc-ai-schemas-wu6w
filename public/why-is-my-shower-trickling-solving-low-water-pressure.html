Why is My Shower Trickling? Solving Low Water Pressure

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

There's nothing quite as frustrating as stepping into the shower, expecting a strong, refreshing spray, and getting a sad trickle instead. Or trying to wash dishes and feeling like the water just dribbles out. Low water pressure isn't just an annoyance; it can signal a few different issues within your plumbing system, and it definitely impacts your daily routine.

Understanding what might be causing it is the first step to getting that powerful flow back. Sometimes, it's as simple as a clogged aerator or showerhead, which you might be able to clean yourself. However, it can also point to bigger problems like mineral buildup in your pipes, especially in older homes, which restricts water flow over time.

A faulty pressure regulator is another common culprit; these devices control the water pressure entering your home, and if they fail, your pressure can drop significantly. There could also be a hidden leak somewhere in your system, which allows water to escape before it reaches your fixtures, leading to reduced pressure. Lastly, issues with your main water supply line or even municipal water problems can be the cause.

Trying to diagnose these complex issues on your own can be tough and often requires specialized tools and expertise.
