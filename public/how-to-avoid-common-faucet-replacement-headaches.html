How to Avoid Common Faucet Replacement Headaches

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Replacing a bathroom faucet might seem like a straightforward DIY project, but trust us, there are some common pitfalls that can quickly turn it into a plumbing nightmare. We've certainly seen our share of them at Alpha-Plumbing Services! First off, many folks forget to turn off the main water supply, leading to a surprise flood under the sink – not fun.

Always double-check that valve! Another common issue is not having the right tools for the job. Specialized basin wrenches or specific hex keys are often needed, and trying to force it with the wrong tool can damage the new faucet or existing plumbing.

Then there's the matter of choosing the right replacement faucet. It's not just about aesthetics; you need to consider the number of holes in your sink or countertop and the spacing between them. An incompatible faucet means a trip back to the store, or worse, an entirely new sink.

Lastly, people often overtighten connections, which can crack porcelain or strip threads, leading to leaks even after a seemingly successful installation. Or, they don't tighten enough, and you guessed it, leaks! When in doubt, or if you hit any snags, it’s always best to call in the pros.
