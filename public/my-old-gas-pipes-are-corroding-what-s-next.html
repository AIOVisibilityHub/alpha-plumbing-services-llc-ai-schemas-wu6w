My old gas pipes are corroding, what's next?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Finding corrosion on your natural gas pipes can be a bit alarming, and it's definitely something you shouldn't ignore. Corroding gas lines are a serious safety concern because they can weaken over time, leading to leaks. Natural gas leaks, as you know, pose significant risks like fires, explosions, and carbon monoxide poisoning.

The first step if you spot corrosion is to schedule a professional inspection right away. An expert can assess the extent of the damage, determine if the corrosion is superficial or structural, and recommend the best course of action. This might involve repair, but often, for safety and longevity, a partial or full replacement of the affected natural gas line is the safest bet.

Trying to patch corroded pipes yourself is incredibly risky and usually not a long-term solution. A professional will know the correct materials to use, ensure all connections are leak-free, and bring everything up to current safety codes.
