Signs You Need a New Bathroom Faucet

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Sometimes, a simple repair can fix a leaky faucet, but other times, it's a clear sign that a replacement is needed. Knowing the difference can save you time and money. Here at Alpha-Plumbing Services, we help homeowners in Goodyear and the surrounding West Valley areas make these decisions every day.

If your faucet is constantly dripping, even after trying to replace washers, it's a big red flag. Persistent leaks not only waste water but can lead to mold growth and water damage to your cabinetry. Another tell-tale sign is visible corrosion or rust, especially around the base or spout.

This isn't just an aesthetic issue; it can compromise the faucet's integrity and indicate deeper problems. Low water pressure specifically from the faucet, not your whole house, can mean mineral buildup that's just too severe to clean effectively. Plus, if your faucet is really old, parts might be hard to find, making repair impractical.

And let’s be honest, an outdated faucet can really detract from your bathroom’s look! Upgrading can significantly improve both function and style. If you’re seeing these signs, it's probably time to consider a new faucet.
