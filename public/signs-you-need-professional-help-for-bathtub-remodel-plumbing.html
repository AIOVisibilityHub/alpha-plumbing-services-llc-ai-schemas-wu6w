Signs You Need Professional Help for Bathtub Remodel Plumbing

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting ready for a bathtub remodel is exciting, but let's be real – the plumbing part can be a bit daunting! It's not always just a simple swap, and sometimes you really need an expert eye. At Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC, we've got over two decades of experience, so we know when it’s time to call in the pros for your bathtub plumbing in Goodyear and surrounding West Valley areas.

One big signal is if your remodel involves moving the tub to a new spot. This isn't just disconnecting and reconnecting; it means rerouting drain lines and water supply lines, which is complex and needs to meet specific codes to avoid future issues. If you’re also upgrading to a much larger tub, a jetted tub, or a rainfall shower system, the existing plumbing might not be designed for the increased water flow or weight. This could require pipe upgrades or even a new water heater, which definitely calls for a professional assessment.

Another red flag is encountering unexpected problems during demolition, like corroded pipes, signs of water damage, or mold. These aren't cosmetic fixes; they're structural and health concerns that need immediate, expert attention before any new fixtures go in. Also, if your home is older, the pipes might be galvanized steel or lead, which should ideally be replaced during a major remodel for better water quality and pressure. This is a job best left to experienced plumbers.
