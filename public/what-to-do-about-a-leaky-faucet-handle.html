What to Do About a Leaky Faucet Handle?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A leaky faucet handle can be quite a nuisance, not to mention a potential source of water damage if left unchecked. It's not uncommon for these leaks to start small, but they can quickly escalate. The good news is that often, a leak around the handle is due to a worn-out O-ring or a faulty stem packing.

These small components are essential for creating a watertight seal. Over time, daily use, mineral deposits from hard water, and general wear and tear can cause them to deteriorate. Trying a DIY fix can sometimes make things worse if you're not familiar with your specific faucet model or the right tools.

Different faucets have different internal mechanisms, and using too much force or the wrong replacement part could lead to further damage. If you're noticing water pooling around your faucet handle or dripping down into your sink cabinet, it's a clear sign that something needs attention.
