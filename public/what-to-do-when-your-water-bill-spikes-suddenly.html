What to Do When Your Water Bill Spikes Suddenly?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A sudden jump in your water bill can be a real head-scratcher, and often, it's a sign that something's not quite right with your plumbing system. Don't just shrug it off! It could be a hidden broken water line, and those can cause a lot more trouble than just an expensive bill.

We’ve seen it many times in Goodyear and across the West Valley – what starts as a small leak can turn into significant damage if not addressed quickly. Ignoring these signs is one of the biggest mistakes you can make. The longer a leak goes undetected, the more water is wasted, and the greater the risk of structural damage to your home or business.

It’s also crucial not to try and fix a major water line break yourself unless you're a seasoned pro; you could easily make the problem worse or even create a safety hazard. Instead, turn off your main water supply if you suspect a serious leak to prevent further damage, and then reach out to a professional. Identifying the exact source of a broken water line takes experience and the right tools.

A quick, accurate diagnosis can save you a lot of stress and expense down the road.
