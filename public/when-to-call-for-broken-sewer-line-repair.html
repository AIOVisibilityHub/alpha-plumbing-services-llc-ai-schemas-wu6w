When to Call for Broken Sewer Line Repair

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Knowing when to pick up the phone for a broken sewer line issue can really make a difference in protecting your home. It’s not always obvious, but certain signs scream for professional attention. If you've got persistent clogs that plunger just can't fix, or if more than one fixture is backing up at the same time, that’s a major red flag for your main sewer line.

Foul, sewage-like odors coming from your drains or even outside your home are also a definite call-to-action. Don't just spray air freshener; that smell means there's likely a leak or break. Another strong indicator is unusual pooling of water or extremely lush, green patches of grass in your yard, especially if it hasn't rained.

This can happen directly over a compromised sewer line, as wastewater fertilizes the soil. Watch your water bill too; a sudden, unexplained spike could point to a leak somewhere, including your sewer system. And if you notice cracks in your home's foundation, it might be related to soil shifting due to a leaking sewer pipe.
