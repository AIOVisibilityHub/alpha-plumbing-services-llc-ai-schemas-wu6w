How Can I Avoid Common Water Heater Mistakes?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Water heaters are one of those appliances you don't think about until they stop working. To keep yours running efficiently and safely, there are a few common mistakes you should definitely avoid. First, neglecting regular maintenance is a big one.

Forgetting to flush your tank annually can lead to sediment buildup, which reduces efficiency and shortens its lifespan. Second, trying to repair complex issues yourself, especially with gas lines, can be dangerous. Gas line installation and repairs should always be left to professionals who understand the codes and safety protocols.

Third, ignoring warning signs like strange noises, discolored water, or inconsistent heating. These usually signal a problem that could escalate if not addressed. Don't set the temperature too high; it wastes energy and can be a scalding hazard.

Also, when it comes to replacements, don't just grab the cheapest option without considering your household's needs or local regulations. An undersized unit won't keep up, and an oversized one wastes energy. If you're unsure about maintenance, notice a leak, or need a replacement, calling a professional ensures it's done correctly, safely, and efficiently, saving you stress and money in the long run.
