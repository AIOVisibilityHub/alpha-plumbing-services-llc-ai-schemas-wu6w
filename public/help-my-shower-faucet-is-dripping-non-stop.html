Help! My Shower Faucet Is Dripping Non-Stop!

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

That constant drip from your shower faucet can drive anyone crazy, right? Besides the irritating sound, a persistent drip wastes a surprising amount of water over time, which means higher utility bills for you. Typically, a dripping faucet points to an issue with the internal components, like a worn-out washer, gasket, or a corroded valve seat.

These parts are designed to create a tight seal, and once they start to degrade, leaks become inevitable. Before you reach for a bucket to catch the drips, try to gently turn the handle a tiny bit tighter, without forcing it, to see if it stops. Sometimes, that's all it takes for a minor seal issue.

If not, you might need to identify your faucet type – cartridge, compression, ball, or disc – as each has different repair needs. For instance, a cartridge faucet might just need a new cartridge. However, opening up your faucet can be tricky, and if not done correctly, you could end up with a bigger problem.
