When Should You Call a Pro for That Dripping Faucet?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

That constant drip, drip, drip can be more than just annoying; it often signals a bigger plumbing issue that shouldn't be ignored. While a simple washer replacement might seem like an easy fix, sometimes the problem runs deeper, involving worn-out valve seats or even pressure imbalances in your system. Trying to tackle a complex repair yourself without the right tools or experience can easily turn a small drip into a much larger, more expensive mess.

It’s not just about stopping the noise; it's about protecting your home from potential water damage and preventing your water bill from creeping up. Don't let a minor inconvenience become a major headache. If you've tried basic troubleshooting and the drip persists, or if you're not comfortable getting under the sink, it's definitely time to bring in the experts.
