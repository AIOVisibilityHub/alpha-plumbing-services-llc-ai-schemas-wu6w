When to Call a Professional for Drain Backup Repair

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A drain backup is one of those household emergencies that can quickly turn into a real nightmare, spreading water and waste where you definitely don't want it. Knowing when to stop trying to fix it yourself and call in the experts from Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC is crucial to preventing extensive damage. We serve Goodyear and surrounding areas like Buckeye and Surprise, and we're ready to help.

  • Multiple fixtures backing up: If not just one, but several drains (like a toilet, sink, and shower) are all backing up at once, this is a clear sign of a main sewer line blockage. This is beyond a simple home repair and requires specialized equipment and expertise to clear safely and effectively.
  • Sewage smell or visible sewage: Any raw sewage smell or the presence of actual sewage backing up into your sinks, tubs, or toilets is a serious health hazard. This situation requires immediate professional attention to protect your family and your home from contaminants.
  • Water backing up in lower-level drains when using upper-level fixtures: If flushing an upstairs toilet causes water to bubble up in a downstairs shower or drain, it's a definite indicator of a significant blockage in your main sewer line. The wastewater has nowhere to go but the path of least resistance.
  • No success with basic plunging: You've given the plunger a good workout, but the water still isn't draining, or it keeps backing up. This means the clog is too deep, too stubborn, or too complex for conventional methods. Professionals have industrial-strength augers and hydro-jetting equipment that can tackle even the toughest blockages.
  • Suspected pipe damage: If you suspect the backup is due to a broken or collapsed pipe, perhaps from tree roots or ground shifting, you'll need professional diagnostic tools like a sewer camera inspection. This isn't something you can eyeball yourself, and DIY attempts could worsen the damage.
