What to Do When Your Water Heater Stops Delivering Hot Water

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Waking up to a cold shower is never fun, and a lack of hot water usually signals a problem with your water heater. Before you panic, check a few things. First, make sure the pilot light (for gas heaters) or circuit breaker (for electric) hasn't tripped or gone out – sometimes it's an easy fix.

Another mistake people make is trying to adjust the thermostat without understanding the proper settings, which can lead to scalding water or still no hot water. Don't ignore strange noises like rumbling or popping, or any signs of leaks around the unit; these are indicators of sediment buildup or tank corrosion that need immediate attention. Attempting complex repairs on your own, especially involving gas lines or electrical components, can be dangerous and should be left to trained professionals.
