Help! My Toilet Is Overflowing – What Do I Do First?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

An overflowing toilet is definitely a stressful situation, but don't panic! The good news is that most of the time, you can stop the overflow pretty quickly if you know what to do. The very first thing is to lift the tank lid and push the flapper down to stop water from entering the bowl.

If that doesn't work, reach for the fill valve float. Lifting the float arm can often temporarily stop the water flow. Once you've got the immediate flood under control, turn off the water supply valve usually located behind or near the base of the toilet.

This will prevent any more water from coming into the tank or bowl. These steps are super important for minimizing water damage to your home, especially if it's happening rapidly. You might think about plunging next, but if it's already overflowing, adding more force could make it worse.

If these initial steps don't resolve the issue and the toilet still seems like it wants to overflow, it's really best to call in a professional.
