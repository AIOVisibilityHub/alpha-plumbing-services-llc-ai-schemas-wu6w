When Your Drain Has a Funky Odor You Can't Get Rid Of

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ever notice a weird, funky smell wafting up from your kitchen or bathroom drain? It's not just unpleasant; it could be a sign of something more serious brewing down there. Most of us try a quick fix first, like pouring some hot water or a little baking soda down, but sometimes that just isn't enough.

If that smell keeps coming back or gets worse, it’s probably time to dig a little deeper. Often, these odors are caused by a build-up of grease, food particles, hair, or even soap scum that creates a hospitable environment for bacteria. Eventually, this gunk can restrict water flow and create a really persistent stench.

Don't let it fester! Ignoring it often means the problem gets tougher to clean later. Sometimes, the issue isn't even in the visible part of the drain but much further down in your pipes.

When household remedies aren't cutting it, or you're starting to notice slower drainage along with the smell, it's a good idea to bring in someone with the right tools and expertise.
