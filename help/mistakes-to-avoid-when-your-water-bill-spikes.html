Mistakes to Avoid When Your Water Bill Spikes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When your water bill suddenly skyrockets, it's easy to panic or jump to conclusions. However, there are a few common mistakes folks make that can actually prolong the issue or even make it worse. Firstly, don't ignore it, hoping it will resolve itself.

Leaks rarely fix themselves and can lead to extensive damage and even higher bills. Secondly, avoid trying complex DIY fixes if you're not sure about the source of the problem. You might inadvertently cause more damage or miss the real culprit, like a deeper pipe issue.

Thirdly, don't assume the utility company made a mistake without doing your own basic checks first; while errors happen, leaks are more common. Fourth, neglecting to check outdoor plumbing like irrigation systems or pool equipment. These can often be huge water hogs.

Finally, don't delay calling a professional if you can't find an obvious cause.
