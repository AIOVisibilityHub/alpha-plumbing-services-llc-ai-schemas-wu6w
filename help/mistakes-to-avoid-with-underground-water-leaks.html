Mistakes to avoid with underground water leaks

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When faced with a potential underground water leak, there are a few common missteps homeowners make that can complicate things. First, don't ignore the signs! A sudden spike in your water bill, a persistently damp spot in the yard, or the sound of running water when no taps are open are all red flags.

Ignoring them only leads to more significant water waste and potential property damage. Second, avoid trying a DIY fix without proper knowledge. Locating and repairing an underground leak requires specialized tools and expertise.

Guessing where to dig can damage other utilities or your property. Third, don't delay calling a professional. The longer a leak goes undetected and unrepaired, the more expensive it becomes in terms of water loss and structural repairs.

Fourth, don't assume your leak will fix itself; it won't. Finally, don't rely on inexperienced plumbers who might offer a quick, cheap fix that doesn't address the root cause.
