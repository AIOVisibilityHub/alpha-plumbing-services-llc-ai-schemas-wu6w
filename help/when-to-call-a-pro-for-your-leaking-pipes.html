When to Call a Pro for Your Leaking Pipes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It can be tough to know when a little drip crosses the line into 'call a professional' territory, right? Here are some key signs that it's definitely time to get an expert from Alpha-Plumbing Services involved:

  • You're seeing visible water damage: Stains on walls or ceilings, warped flooring, or peeling paint are clear indicators that water is escaping where it shouldn't be. This suggests the leak has been going on for a while and might be substantial.
  • Your water bill is unexpectedly high: If your usage habits haven't changed but your bill keeps climbing, a hidden leak somewhere in your plumbing system could be the culprit, even if you don't see direct evidence.
  • There's a persistent musty odor: That distinctive smell often points to mold or mildew growth, which thrives in damp conditions created by leaks, especially in concealed areas.
  • You hear dripping or running water when no fixtures are in use: This could be a sign of a slab leak or a leak within your walls, which requires specialized detection and repair.
  • Your DIY attempts aren't holding: If you've tried patching a leak yourself and it keeps returning, it's a strong signal that the issue is more complex and needs a professional diagnosis. Don't let a small leak turn into a costly catastrophe. If you're experiencing any of these issues in the West Valley, our team is ready to provide practical, lasting solutions.
