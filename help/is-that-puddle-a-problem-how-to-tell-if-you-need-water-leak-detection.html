Is That Puddle a Problem? How to Tell if You Need Water Leak Detection

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Finding an unexpected puddle can be alarming, right? Sometimes it’s just a spilled drink, but other times it’s a sign of something much bigger. Don't panic, but don't ignore it either!

Early water leak detection can save you a ton of stress and cash down the road. You might be wondering, how do you even know if it's a real plumbing leak or just a fluke? Well, there are some pretty clear signs that it’s time to get a professional involved to pinpoint the issue.

Look for persistent damp spots, especially on ceilings, walls, or under sinks – even small ones. If your water bill suddenly spikes without any change in usage, that's a huge red flag for a hidden leak. You should also pay attention to any musty odors or visible mold growth, as these often indicate ongoing moisture problems behind walls or floors.

Don't forget to check your water meter; if it's still spinning when all water is off, you've definitely got a leak somewhere. A damp spot on the concrete slab can mean a slab leak, which needs immediate attention to prevent structural damage. Ignoring these signs can lead to significant damage to your home’s structure and your wallet.
