When to Call for Help with Water Leaks Under Your House

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Dealing with a water leak under your house is a situation no homeowner wants to face, but knowing exactly when to pick up the phone and call a professional can make all the difference. These types of leaks aren't just annoying; they can lead to some seriously expensive issues if not caught and repaired promptly. You’ll want to be on the lookout for a few clear signs that indicate it’s time for expert intervention.

One of the most obvious signals is an unexpected surge in your water bill, even if your usage hasn't changed. This is often the first clue that water is escaping somewhere underground. Another tell-tale sign is hearing the sound of running water when all taps and appliances are off – that usually means there's a leak within your plumbing system.

Be sure to also check for unexplained damp spots on your concrete slab, baseboards, or even in your yard, especially if there are areas that are unusually lush or soggy. Drops in water pressure can also point to a leak somewhere in your main line. If you're noticing any persistent musty odors, particularly in lower levels or specific rooms, mold growth due to a hidden leak could be the culprit.
