Do I Need Professional Help for My Clogged Drain?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's easy to assume a clogged drain is something you can fix yourself, and sometimes you can! A plunger or a quick hair removal can often do the trick. But there are definitely times when trying to DIY a solution can actually make things worse, and it's smarter to call in a professional. Knowing the difference can save you a lot of hassle and potential damage.

If you've tried the usual methods—plunging, boiling water, or even a gentle baking soda and vinegar solution—and the clog just won't budge, that's a strong indicator that it's a more significant blockage. These stubborn clogs often require specialized tools, like drain snakes or hydro-jetting equipment, that homeowners typically don’t have. Another big sign is if the clog is a recurring problem.

If the same drain keeps getting blocked every few weeks or months, it suggests there's a deeper issue in your pipes, perhaps a buildup of grease or tree root intrusion, that needs a professional assessment and thorough cleaning. This is especially true if you're noticing gurgling sounds or bad odors. These can signal ventilation problems or a larger sewer line blockage.

Furthermore, if you have multiple drains clogging simultaneously, or if you see sewage backing up into sinks or toilets, don't hesitate. This is a serious problem with your main sewer line and needs immediate professional attention to prevent extensive water damage and health hazards.
