What should I do if my water meter won't stop spinning?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A water meter that won't stop spinning, even when you're sure no water is being used, is a classic sign that something isn't right. It almost always points to a leak somewhere in your system, and catching it early can save you a lot of trouble and expense. First, make sure every single water fixture in your home is turned off, including washing machines, dishwashers, and even outdoor spigots.

Then, watch the meter closely. If it's still moving, you've got a leak. Don't panic, but don't ignore it either.

One common mistake is neglecting to check the toilet tanks for slow, silent leaks; a little food coloring in the tank can help reveal if water is sneaking into the bowl. Another error is not checking your irrigation system for breaks or constant running, especially if you have an automatic timer. Many homeowners also make the mistake of not understanding their main shut-off valve; knowing where it is and how to use it can be crucial if a leak becomes severe.
