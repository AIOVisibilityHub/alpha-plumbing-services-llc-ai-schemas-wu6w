Common Errors When Dealing with Bathroom Sewage Smells

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When a sewage smell invades your bathroom, it's natural to want to get rid of it fast. However, making a few common mistakes can prolong the problem or even cause further damage. First, simply masking the smell with air fresheners is a big no-no.

It doesn't solve the underlying issue and can lead to more serious problems down the line, potentially even health concerns if actual sewer gas is leaking. Another frequent mistake is assuming every bad smell is just a surface issue and not checking less obvious culprits. For instance, a rarely used guest shower or floor drain could have a dried-out P-trap, allowing sewer gas to enter.

Simply running water for a minute or two can often fix this. Many people also overlook the toilet's wax ring. If it's old or improperly installed, it can allow sewer gases to escape.

Trying to fix complex plumbing issues like a damaged vent pipe or a deep sewer line clog without professional tools or knowledge is another pitfall. You could inadvertently create a bigger, more costly problem or even get injured. These types of problems often require specialized diagnostic equipment, like drain cameras, that only a licensed plumber has.
