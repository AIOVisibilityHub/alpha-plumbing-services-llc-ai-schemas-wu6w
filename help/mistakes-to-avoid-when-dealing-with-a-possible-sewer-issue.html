Mistakes to Avoid When Dealing With a Possible Sewer Issue

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you suspect a sewer line issue, it’s easy to panic and make some common mistakes that can actually worsen the problem or lead to bigger headaches. Knowing what not to do is just as important as knowing what to do! First, don't ignore the early signs.

Those slow drains, gurgling noises, or faint odors aren't going to fix themselves. Ignoring them often leads to a full-blown backup or burst pipe, which is much messier and more expensive. Second, avoid blindly digging in your yard.

You could easily hit other utility lines, causing more damage or even dangerous situations. Professional sewer line locating uses precise tools to find the problem without tearing up your whole property. Third, don't overuse harsh chemical drain cleaners.

While they seem like a quick fix, they can corrode your pipes, especially older ones, leading to leaks and breaks over time. They rarely solve the root cause of a sewer line clog anyway. Fourth, don't assume a DIY fix will work for a major sewer line problem.

These systems are complex, and a temporary patch won't address underlying issues like root intrusion or collapsed pipes. You might just be delaying a bigger, more urgent repair. Fifth, don't hesitate to call a professional if you're unsure.

Guessing games with your home's plumbing can be incredibly costly. Experts like us have the diagnostic tools and experience to identify the exact problem and recommend the right solution.
