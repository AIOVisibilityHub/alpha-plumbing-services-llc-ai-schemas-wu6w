How to Deal with a Malfunctioning Pool Heater Gas Line

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

If your pool heater’s gas line seems to be acting up, it can definitely put a damper on your swimming plans. The first thing to do is prioritize safety. If you smell gas or hear a hissing sound, immediately turn off the gas supply to the heater if you know how to do so safely, then evacuate the area and call for professional help.

Don't try to ignite the heater or use any electrical switches. If there’s no immediate danger like a strong gas smell but your heater just isn’t working, you can check a few simple things. Make sure the gas valve to the heater is fully open.

Sometimes, it’s just a simple oversight. Also, check your thermostat settings to ensure it’s calling for heat. However, for anything related to the gas line itself, it's really best to call in experts.
