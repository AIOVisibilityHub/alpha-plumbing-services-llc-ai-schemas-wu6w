Common Mistakes When Dealing with a Noisy Water Heater

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Dealing with a noisy water heater can be frustrating, and it’s easy to make a few common missteps that can prolong the problem or even create bigger ones. One major mistake is ignoring the noise, hoping it'll just go away. Those rumbling or popping sounds often mean sediment buildup, and if left unchecked, that sediment can reduce efficiency and even lead to premature tank failure.

Don't assume all noises are normal; persistent or increasingly loud sounds are usually trying to tell you something. Another common error is attempting a DIY fix without really understanding the cause. While a simple drain and flush can sometimes help with sediment, if the noise is due to a faulty heating element or internal pressure issues, you could do more harm than good without the right expertise.

People also sometimes forget to consider the age of their water heater. Older units are more prone to these issues, and sometimes, a replacement is simply the most cost-effective and reliable solution rather than continuous repairs. Lastly, not calling a professional when the noises are accompanied by other symptoms like rusty water, a lack of hot water, or, worst of all, a leak, is a big mistake.
