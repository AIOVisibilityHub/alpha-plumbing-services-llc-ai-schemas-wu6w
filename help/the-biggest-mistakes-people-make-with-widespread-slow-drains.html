The Biggest Mistakes People Make with Widespread Slow Drains

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When all the drains in your house start acting up, it's easy to get frustrated and try a quick fix. But believe it or not, there are some common mistakes folks make that can actually worsen the problem or lead to bigger headaches down the line. It's important to know what not to do when you're dealing with sluggish plumbing throughout your entire home.

One big mistake is ignoring the problem entirely. A single slow drain might be an annoyance, but when it's widespread, it's a red flag for a main sewer line issue. Letting it go can lead to severe backups, property damage, and much more expensive repairs.

Another common misstep is using harsh chemical drain cleaners repeatedly. While they might seem to offer a temporary fix, these chemicals are corrosive and can damage your pipes, especially older ones, leading to leaks or collapses over time. Plus, they rarely address the root cause of a main line blockage.

Mistake number three is trying to force a stubborn clog with excessive pressure or improper tools. You could easily damage your pipes or seals, turning a clog into a much larger plumbing emergency.

Another thing we see is people attempting to snake the main line themselves without the proper equipment or experience. Main line clogs are serious and require specific tools and know-how to clear effectively and safely. You might push the clog further or damage the line. Finally, don't forget to consider what you're flushing. Wipes, paper towels, and excessive food scraps can accumulate and cause significant blockages that affect the entire system. Understanding what not to do is just as important as knowing what to do.
