Mistakes to Avoid When Dealing with Underground Pipes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Dealing with underground plumbing can feel a bit like working blind, and that's where mistakes can really happen. Here are a few common pitfalls to avoid when you're thinking about projects involving your subterranean drain lines. First, never assume you know where your pipes are just by memory or old plans; things can shift, and plans aren't always 100% accurate.

Relying on outdated information can lead to accidentally hitting a line during excavation, which is a mess you absolutely want to avoid. Another mistake is starting any major digging project without having your underground utilities, including drain lines, professionally marked. It’s not just about convenience; it’s about safety and preventing costly damage.

Also, don't ignore signs of a potential underground issue, like soggy spots in your yard or sudden increases in your water bill, thinking they'll go away. These could indicate a leak or break that needs professional attention and precise locating. Finally, trying to DIY a major pipe repair without knowing the exact location and nature of the problem is a big gamble.
