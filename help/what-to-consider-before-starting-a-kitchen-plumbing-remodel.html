What to Consider Before Starting a Kitchen Plumbing Remodel

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about a kitchen remodel? That’s fantastic! The kitchen is often the heart of the home, and upgrading it can make a huge difference in daily life. But before you dive into picking out countertops and cabinets, it’s super important to think about the plumbing. Getting it right from the start means fewer headaches and a kitchen that truly works for you.

First up, consider your current layout and what you want to change. Are you just swapping out the sink, or are you dreaming of moving the dishwasher to a new spot, adding a prep sink on an island, or even installing a pot filler over the stove? Each of these changes means adjusting water supply lines and drains, which can be a bigger job than you might think.

Second, think about appliance upgrades. If you’re getting a new refrigerator with an ice maker or a more powerful dishwasher, you’ll need to ensure the plumbing connections are appropriate and up to snuff. A water softener installation might also be something to consider for your new kitchen, especially with Arizona's hard water.

Third, don't forget about your water heater. If your kitchen remodel involves adding more fixtures that will use hot water, you might need to assess if your current water heater can handle the increased demand or if an upgrade, perhaps to a tankless system, is a better long-term solution. Fourth, always budget for professional help.

While it might be tempting to DIY some parts, critical elements like gas line installation (if you’re switching to a gas range) or major drain re-routing are jobs for experienced professionals like us at Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC. We ensure everything is up to International Plumbing Code standards. Lastly, plan for the future.

Will your family grow? Do you entertain often? Thinking about these long-term needs can help you design a plumbing system that will serve you well for years to come.
