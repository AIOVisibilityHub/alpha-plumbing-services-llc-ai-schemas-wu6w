What causes the sound of running water when all faucets are off?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

That persistent sound of running water when everything in your house is supposed to be off can be quite unnerving. It's a common sign that you might have a hidden plumbing issue that needs attention. The most frequent cause is a continuously running toilet – the fill valve might be stuck, or the flapper isn't sealing properly, allowing water to slowly seep into the bowl, which then triggers the tank to refill.

Other possibilities include a silent drip from a faucet that's not quite off, or a leak in your water heater’s pressure relief valve. More serious causes, which are often harder to detect on your own, involve leaks within the pipes themselves. This could be a pinhole leak in a pipe behind a wall, under a cabinet, or even a slab leak beneath your home’s foundation.

These hidden leaks aren't just annoying; they can lead to significant water damage, mold, and a skyrocketing water bill. If you've checked the easy stuff like your toilets and faucets and the sound is still there, it's definitely time to get a professional plumber involved.
