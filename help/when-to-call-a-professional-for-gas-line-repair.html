When to Call a Professional for Gas Line Repair

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Knowing when to pick up the phone for a gas line repair can literally save lives. It's not something to gamble with. Here are some clear signs that it's time to call in the experts at Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC:

  • You smell gas: That distinct rotten egg odor is mercaptan, and it means gas is escaping. Don't ignore it, even if it's faint.
  • You hear a hissing sound: This often indicates a pressurized gas leak from a pipe or appliance.
  • Your gas bill suddenly spikes: An unexplained increase can sometimes point to a leak that’s slowly bleeding gas.
  • Dead or dying plants near gas lines: Gas leaks in the ground can affect vegetation around your property.
  • Damaged gas pipe visible: If you see any physical damage to an exposed gas pipe, call immediately.
  • Pilot light keeps going out: While sometimes a minor appliance issue, it can also indicate gas supply problems or a leak.

If you notice any of these, don't hesitate.
