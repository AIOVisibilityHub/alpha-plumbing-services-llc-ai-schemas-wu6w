When to Call a Professional for a Leaking Toilet Base

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Seeing water seep out from the base of your toilet is a clear sign that something’s amiss, and while some minor plumbing issues can be DIY projects, a leaking toilet base often warrants a call to the pros. It's usually more involved than tightening a bolt and can lead to significant water damage if not handled correctly.

You should definitely call a professional if you’ve noticed the leak persists after trying basic troubleshooting, like gently tightening the toilet bolts or checking for obvious cracks. If the leak is consistent with every flush, it almost certainly indicates a failed wax ring that needs replacement, which involves removing the toilet – a job that can be heavy and tricky to do right without experience. Any visible damage to your bathroom floor, like soft spots, discoloration, or a persistent musty smell, means you’ve already got water damage, and a professional needs to assess the extent and ensure it's properly repaired.

Also, if your toilet feels wobbly or unstable, that movement can repeatedly break the wax seal, making a professional installation of a new wax ring and proper toilet securing essential.
