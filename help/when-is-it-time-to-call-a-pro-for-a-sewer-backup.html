When is it Time to Call a Pro for a Sewer Backup?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A sewer backup can be a real headache, and honestly, a bit gross. Sometimes it's a minor clog you can handle yourself, but how do you know when it's time to call in Alpha-Plumbing Services? There are definitely some tell-tale signs.

If you notice multiple drains in your home are slow or backed up simultaneously, like your toilet, shower, and sink all acting up at once, that's a big red flag. Another signal is foul odors coming from your drains or even your yard; this suggests sewage might be pooling where it shouldn't be. Gurgling sounds from your pipes, especially when you flush a toilet or run water elsewhere, also point to a deeper issue.

And of course, visible sewage coming up through drains or into your basement is an immediate emergency that needs professional attention. Don't delay on these; sewer problems can lead to serious water damage and health risks.
