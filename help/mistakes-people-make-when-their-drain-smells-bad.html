Mistakes People Make When Their Drain Smells Bad

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's easy to panic when your drain starts smelling terrible, but a few common missteps can actually make things worse. First, a big mistake is ignoring the smell. It might seem minor, but it usually signals a buildup that will only worsen over time, leading to more stubborn clogs and stronger odors.

Another common error is pouring harsh chemical drain cleaners down the pipe. These can sometimes offer a temporary fix for minor clogs, but they're incredibly corrosive and can damage your plumbing system, especially older pipes. Plus, they rarely address the root cause of a persistent odor.

Don't forget that if the smell is from the garbage disposal, you might need to clean the disposal itself, not just the drain. Simply running water through it often isn't enough; try ice, rock salt, or citrus peels. Also, many folks repeatedly try the same home remedies (like baking soda and vinegar) without success.

While these can work for minor issues, if the smell persists after a few tries, you're likely dealing with something that requires professional attention. Lastly, assuming it's 'just a little gunk' and not considering a bigger issue like a partially blocked vent pipe or a dried-out P-trap is a miss. For situations where the odor is stubborn or you're unsure of the cause, it's really best to get a professional evaluation.
