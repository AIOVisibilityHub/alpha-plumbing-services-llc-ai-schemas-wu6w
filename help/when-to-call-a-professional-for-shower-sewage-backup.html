When to Call a Professional for Shower Sewage Backup

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It’s a truly unpleasant experience when you find sewage backing up into your shower, and it’s usually a clear sign that you need professional help, fast. This isn't just a simple clogged drain; it indicates a more serious issue with your main sewer line. Here are some definite signs it’s time to pick up the phone:

  • Multiple fixtures are backing up: If your toilets, sinks, and showers are all struggling, it's almost certainly a main line problem.
  • Sewage coming up: Any appearance of raw sewage, whether in your shower, tub, or toilet, signals an urgent and unsanitary situation.
  • Unpleasant odors persist: A lingering, foul, sewer-like smell that won't go away often points to a break or blockage in your sewer system.
  • Slow drainage across your home: If all your drains are suddenly sluggish, it’s a system-wide issue, not just a local clog.
  • Gurgling sounds from drains: These noises often indicate air being trapped by a blockage.
