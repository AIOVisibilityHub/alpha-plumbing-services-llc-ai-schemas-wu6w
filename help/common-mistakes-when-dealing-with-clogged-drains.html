Common Mistakes When Dealing with Clogged Drains

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When your drain starts acting up, it's easy to jump to quick fixes, but some common reactions can actually make things worse or just delay the inevitable. Knowing what not to do can save you a lot of headache and potentially costly repairs down the line. Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC has seen it all across Goodyear and the West Valley, so here's a few pointers on what to avoid.

  • Pouring harsh chemical drain cleaners: These can seem like a magic bullet, but they're often bad news. Many store-bought cleaners contain highly corrosive chemicals that can damage your pipes, especially older ones, and they don't always clear the entire clog. Plus, if they don't work, you're left with caustic standing water that's dangerous for anyone trying to fix it afterward.
  • Over-relying on plungers for recurring clogs: A plunger is great for a simple, superficial clog. But if you're constantly plunging the same drain, it's a sign of a deeper issue. Repeated, aggressive plunging can sometimes worsen a main line clog or even damage pipe seals.
  • Ignoring slow drains: A slow drain isn't just annoying; it's often an early warning sign of a developing blockage. Procrastinating can allow the clog to become more severe and much harder to remove later.
  • Using improper tools: Shoving a wire hanger down the drain might seem like a good idea, but you risk pushing the clog further down, scratching the inside of your pipes, or even puncturing a pipe if you're too aggressive. Leave the specialized tools to the professionals.
  • Not understanding the cause: If you don't know why your drain is clogging, you're just putting a band-aid on the problem. A professional plumber can properly diagnose the root cause, whether it's grease buildup, tree roots, or a damaged pipe, and provide a lasting solution.
