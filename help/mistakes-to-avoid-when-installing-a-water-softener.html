Mistakes to Avoid When Installing a Water Softener

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about a new water softener for your home? That’s a smart move, especially with the hard water we often see in places like Buckeye and Avondale. But hold on a second – there are a few common slip-ups people make during installation that you’ll want to dodge.

First, don't just guess at the right size; an undersized unit won't keep up, and an oversized one is just a waste. Second, skipping the bypass valve installation is a big no-no; you’ll regret it if you ever need to service the unit. Third, ignoring proper drain line installation can lead to messy backups and potential health hazards.

Also, don't forget to consider future maintenance access – you'll need to refill salt, so make sure it's easily reachable. Finally, trying to DIY a complex installation without the right plumbing know-how can lead to leaks, damage, and voided warranties.
