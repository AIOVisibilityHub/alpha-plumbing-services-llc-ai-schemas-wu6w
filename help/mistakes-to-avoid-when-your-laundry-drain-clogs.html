Mistakes to Avoid When Your Laundry Drain Clogs

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When your laundry drain clogs, it's easy to jump to quick fixes, but sometimes those quick fixes can lead to bigger headaches. Let's chat about some common slip-ups and how to steer clear of them. First, don't ignore slow draining; it's a warning sign!

Addressing it early can prevent a full-blown backup. Second, resist the urge to pour harsh chemical drain cleaners down the pipe. These can be really corrosive, damaging your plumbing, and they often don't fully clear the clog, leaving you with a worse problem or even pipe leaks down the line.

Third, don't try to force a coat hanger or makeshift tool too far into the drain. You might just push the clog deeper or scratch the pipes, creating future snag points. Fourth, repeatedly plunging without success can actually create more pressure on weak pipe joints, potentially causing a leak.

Lastly, don't assume the problem will just go away on its own or that it’s 'just a little clog' if it keeps happening. Recurring clogs often indicate a deeper issue in your plumbing system.
