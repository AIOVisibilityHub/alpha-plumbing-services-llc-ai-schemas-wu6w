Mistakes to Avoid When Your Drains Gurgle

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Hearing gurgling noises from your drains can be pretty alarming, and it's tempting to try and fix it yourself. However, some common DIY mistakes can actually make the problem worse or mask a more serious underlying issue. Knowing what not to do can save you a lot of hassle and expense in the long run. Here are some missteps to steer clear of when those gurgles start echoing through your pipes, especially if you're in areas like Surprise, Waddel, or Glendale.

First, don't ignore it, thinking it will just go away. Gurgling is usually a symptom of a ventilation problem or a developing clog, and it almost always gets worse over time. Second, avoid pouring chemical drain cleaners down gurgling drains.

As we've mentioned before, these aren't a magic bullet and can corrode pipes, especially with ongoing use. They're particularly ineffective for vent issues, which often cause gurgling. Third, don't just try to mask smells with air fresheners if odors accompany the gurgling.

Foul smells signal decomposing organic matter in your pipes or a compromised sewer line, which needs professional cleaning or repair, not just covering up. Fourth, don't assume a gurgle in one drain means the problem is localized there. Gurgling can be a sign of a main line issue, affecting multiple fixtures, and a localized fix won't help.

Lastly, don't try to clear a suspected vent issue on your roof yourself unless you're experienced and have the right safety gear. Climbing on roofs and working with vent pipes can be dangerous and could lead to further damage if not done correctly.

When faced with persistent gurgling, it's often best to consult with experienced professionals.
