Mistakes to Avoid When Dealing with a Clogged Bathtub Drain

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Facing a clogged bathtub drain can be a real headache, and it's tempting to reach for the quickest solution. While many clogs are minor and can be fixed with some DIY elbow grease, there are some common mistakes folks make that can actually turn a simple problem into a much bigger, more expensive mess. It’s all about knowing when to tackle it yourself and when to call in someone with more specialized tools and experience. Avoiding these pitfalls can save your pipes, your wallet, and a whole lot of frustration in the long run.

One of the biggest mistakes is using chemical drain cleaners frequently or as a first resort. While they might seem effective, these harsh chemicals can corrode your pipes over time, especially older ones, and they often don't fully clear tough clogs. Plus, they're not great for the environment or your health.

Another common error is forcing a snake or auger when you encounter resistance. If you push too hard, you risk puncturing or damaging your pipes, which can lead to leaks behind walls or under the tub. Also, many people don't fully remove the hair and gunk they pull out; leaving some behind means the clog will likely reform quickly.

A truly clear drain means a thorough cleaning. Lastly, ignoring persistent or recurring clogs is a major misstep. If your drain keeps clogging after you've tried to clear it, it's a strong indicator of a deeper issue, like a main sewer line problem or a damaged pipe section, that requires professional diagnosis.

If you're dealing with a stubborn clog, or if you've made these mistakes and need a fix, it’s smart to get professional help.
