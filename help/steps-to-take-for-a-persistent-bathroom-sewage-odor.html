Steps to Take for a Persistent Bathroom Sewage Odor

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Finding that persistent, unpleasant sewage odor in your bathroom is a signal that something needs attention, and fast. Tackling it systematically can help you either resolve it or understand when it's time for professional help. Start by identifying if any drains in your bathroom, especially those less frequently used like a guest shower or floor drain, have dried-out P-traps.

Running water in each drain for a minute or two can refill these traps, creating a water seal that blocks sewer gases. Next, give your toilet a good inspection. Check if it wobbles or if there’s any dampness around the base, which could indicate a failing wax ring.

A compromised wax ring is a common entry point for sewer gas. If these basic checks don't solve the problem, or if the smell is very strong and constant, it's likely a more complex issue like a blocked or damaged vent pipe, or even a sewer line problem. These aren't usually DIY fixes, as they require specific diagnostic tools and knowledge to locate and repair safely.

Ignoring a strong, persistent sewage smell is never a good idea; it can indicate issues that affect your home’s air quality and plumbing integrity.
