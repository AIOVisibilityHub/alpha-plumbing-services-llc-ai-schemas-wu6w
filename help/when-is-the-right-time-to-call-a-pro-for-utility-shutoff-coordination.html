When is the Right Time to Call a Pro for Utility Shutoff Coordination?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Dealing with a big plumbing repair often means temporarily shutting off utilities like water or gas. It sounds simple, but coordinating these shutoffs and then getting everything safely restored can be a real headache if you don't do it right. You definitely want to call in the pros when your repair involves anything beyond a basic fixture replacement.

If you're looking at a major pipe repair, a slab leak fix, or even a whole-home repiping, coordinating the utility shutoff with the city or utility company is crucial. This isn't just about turning a valve; it's about making sure everything is done according to local codes and that services are restored safely and efficiently afterward. Trust me, you don't want to be stuck without water or gas longer than necessary because of a coordination mix-up.
