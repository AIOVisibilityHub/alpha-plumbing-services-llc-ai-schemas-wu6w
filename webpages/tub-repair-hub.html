Tub Repair | Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-20

Type: CollectionPage

Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC offers reliable tub repair to fix leaks and drainage issues, helping homeowners avoid water damage and restore their bathroom's functionality. A leaking or damaged bathtub can lead to significant water damage if not addressed quickly. Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC provides professional tub repair services to resolve issues ranging from faulty drains and leaking valves to fixture malfunctions. Our technicians, including David Wasserman, have extensive experience diagnosing tub problems in tract homes, custom builds, and commercial settings across the West Valley.

We understand that a functional bathroom is essential for your daily routine. As a family-operated business, we focus on practical, high-quality repairs that restore your tub's functionality without unnecessary costs. We offer free estimates to help you understand the scope of the repair, ensuring your tub is leak-free and operating correctly in accordance with professional standards. Common situations: Water leaking from the tub drain into the floor or ceiling below.; A bathtub faucet or spout that won't stop dripping or leaking behind the wall.; Low water pressure or temperature issues specifically affecting the bathtub.. Related services: bathtub leak repair, bathtub drain repair, faucet repair and replacement, shower repair, remodel plumbing, plumbing leak repair. Locations served: Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, Sun City West, El Mirage, Glendale, Phoenix.
