Water Heater Replacement and Installation | Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-23

Type: CollectionPage

Reliable water heater replacement and installation services in Goodyear and the West Valley from a family-owned team with 25 years of experience. Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC provides expert water heater solutions for homeowners and businesses throughout Goodyear and the West Valley. With over 25 years of experience, our team, including David and Jamie Wasserman, handles everything from standard tank water heater installations to efficient tankless water heater replacements. We focus on proper installation according to the International Plumbing Code, ensuring your new unit operates safely and reliably.

If you are experiencing no hot water, rusty water, or notice your tank is leaking, we can diagnose the issue and recommend the best replacement option. We also handle gas line repairs and installations specifically for water heaters, coordinating necessary inspections to ensure your system is up to code. Whether you need a direct hot water heater replacement or are looking to upgrade your home’s capacity, we provide quality workmanship at an excellent price. Common situations: The water heater is leaking from the base or tank.; Water temperature is inconsistent or there is no hot water at all.; The unit is making rumbling or knocking noises during operation.; Upgrading to a tankless water heater for better energy efficiency.. Related services: natural gas-line installation, hot water heater replacement, tankless water heater installation, gas line repairs for water heaters, plumbing repair, hard water solutions. Locations served: Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, Waddel, Sun City, Sun City West, Tonapha, Tolleson, Peoria, Glendale, Phoenix, Young Town, El Mirage, Litchfield.
