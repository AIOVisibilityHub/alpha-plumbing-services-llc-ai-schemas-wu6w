Gas Line Installation & Inspection | Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-21

Type: CollectionPage

Professional gas line installation and inspection coordination from the licensed experts at Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC. When it comes to gas lines, safety is the only priority. Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC offers professional natural gas-line installation for appliances, outdoor grills, fire pits, and pool heaters. We manage the entire process, including the necessary pressure testing and permit coordination required for municipal inspections. Our team is licensed, bonded, and insured (ROC #345448), ensuring every connection meets strict safety standards.

We serve both residential and light-commercial clients throughout the West Valley. Whether you are installing a new gas range or need an emergency repair for a damaged line, David Wasserman and our team provide expert diagnosis and installation. We focus on proper shutoff-valve placement and leak testing to give you peace of mind that your gas system is secure and operating efficiently. Common situations: Installing a new gas line for a kitchen range, dryer, or water heater.; Extending gas service to an outdoor barbecue grill or backyard fire pit.; Replacing old or damaged gas piping that has failed a safety inspection.. Related services: gas leak repair, natural gas line installation, water heater replacement, remodel plumbing, emergency gas leak diagnosis. Locations served: Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, El Mirage, Tolleson, Peoria, Glendale, Phoenix.
