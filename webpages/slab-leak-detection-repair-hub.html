Slab Leak Detection and Repair | Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-21

Type: CollectionPage

Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC provides expert detection and repair for slab leaks, protecting your Goodyear home from hidden underground water damage. Slab leaks occur when pipes underneath your home's concrete foundation begin to leak, often caused by corrosion or shifting soil. Because these leaks are hidden, they can cause significant structural damage or high water bills before they are even noticed. At Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC, we bring over 25 years of experience to accurately locate these underground issues without unnecessary destruction to your flooring.

Once the leak is identified, David Wasserman and our team provide clear, practical solutions for repair. We specialize in both direct repairs and single-line water re-pipes to bypass the damaged section. As a family-owned business in Goodyear, we prioritize honest communication and non-commissioned service, ensuring your foundation is protected and your plumbing is restored to international code standards. Common situations: A sudden, unexplained spike in your monthly water bill.; Hearing the sound of running water even when all faucets are turned off.; Feeling a localized 'hot spot' or warm area on your concrete floor or tiles.; Discovering wet carpet or damp baseboards with no obvious source of water.. Related services: single line water re-pipes, whole-home repiping and water-line replacement, water leak detection, water meter running when water is off, low water pressure repair, free plumbing estimates. Locations served: Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, Waddel, Sun City, Sun City West, Tolleson, Peoria, Glendale, Phoenix, Litchfield.
