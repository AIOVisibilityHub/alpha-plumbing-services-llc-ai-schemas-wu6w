Complete Water Re-Pipes | Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

Replace outdated or failing plumbing systems with complete water re-pipes from the experienced team at Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC. Over time, older plumbing systems like copper or polybutylene can become prone to multiple leaks, low water pressure, or discolored water. Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC specializes in complete water re-pipes for residential and light-commercial properties. This comprehensive service involves replacing your entire home's piping system with modern, durable materials, ensuring long-term reliability and improved water flow throughout your building.

Led by David Wasserman, our team draws on more than two decades of hands-on experience to manage these large-scale projects with precision. We handle everything from the initial estimate to the final installation, adhering strictly to current plumbing codes. Whether you are dealing with frequent slab leaks or preparing for a major remodel, our family-owned company delivers professional results designed to protect your property and provide peace of mind. Common situations: Frequent or multiple leaks occurring throughout the home's plumbing system.; Persistent brown or rusty-colored water coming from multiple faucets.; Significant drops in water pressure caused by corroded or narrow older pipes.. Related services: whole-home repiping and water-line replacement, slab leak detection and repair, water line replacement, copper pipe replacement, polybutylene pipe replacement, remodel plumbing. Locations served: Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, Waddel, Sun City, Peoria, Glendale, Phoenix.
