Slab Leaks | Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-21

Type: CollectionPage

Expert slab leak detection and repair services to protect your home's foundation and plumbing system. Slab leaks occur when water lines under your home's concrete foundation begin to fail, often leading to hidden damage. Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC specializes in slab leak detection and repair, using specialized tools to find the exact source of the leak. Signs like a warm spot on the floor, an unexplained high water bill, or the sound of running water when all faucets are off are indicators that you may need professional assistance.

Once a leak is detected, David Wasserman and our experienced team offer several repair options, including direct repairs or single-line water re-routes to bypass the damaged pipe. We provide free estimates and non-commissioned advice, ensuring you get the most durable and cost-effective fix for your home. We are proud to serve the Goodyear community with expert solutions for even the most difficult underground leaks. Common situations: Noticing a hot spot on a concrete floor or damp carpet with no obvious source.; Receiving a high water bill despite no change in daily water usage.; Hearing water running behind walls or under floors when no water is in use.. Related services: water leak detection, whole-home repiping, water line repair, single line water re-pipes, leaking pipe repair. Locations served: Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, Waddel, Litchfield, Peoria, Glendale, Phoenix.
