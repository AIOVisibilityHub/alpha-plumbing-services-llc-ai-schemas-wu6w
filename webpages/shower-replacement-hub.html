Shower Replacement | Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

Upgrade your bathroom with professional shower replacement services from Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC, serving Goodyear and the surrounding West Valley communities. Whether you are updating your bathroom’s look or replacing a damaged unit, Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC provides professional shower replacement services. We handle the entire process, from disconnecting old fixtures to the expert installation of new shower systems. Our team ensures that all connections are secure and that the new unit is properly integrated with your home’s existing plumbing and drainage.

With 25 years of experience in both residential and light-commercial plumbing, David Wasserman and the Alpha-Plumbing team bring a high level of expertise to every bathroom project. We pride ourselves on non-commission-based service, meaning we focus solely on providing the best installation for your specific needs. From standard tract home replacements to custom bathroom remodels, we ensure your new shower is installed to meet all local codes and quality standards. Common situations: Upgrading an old or cracked shower unit during a bathroom remodel.; Replacing a shower that has persistent, non-repairable leaks behind the walls.; Switching from a tub-shower combo to a dedicated walk-in shower.. Related services: shower installation, shower repair, bathroom remodel plumbing, fixture repair and replacement, new fixture installation, bathtub replacement. Locations served: Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, Peoria, Glendale, Phoenix, Tolleson.
