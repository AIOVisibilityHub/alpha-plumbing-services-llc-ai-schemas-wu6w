Water Pressure Too High | Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC provides expert solutions for water pressure that is too high, protecting your Goodyear home's pipes and appliances from unnecessary wear and tear. While high water pressure might seem like a luxury in the shower, it is actually a significant threat to your plumbing system. Excessive pressure puts constant stress on your pipes, joints, and appliances, often leading to premature water heater failure, leaking faucets, and even burst pipes. Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC helps homeowners identify and correct high-pressure issues to prevent long-term damage.

If you hear banging or knocking sounds in your walls (water hammer) or notice your toilets constantly running, your pressure may be set too high. Our team can install or replace pressure-reducing valves and perform system tests to ensure your home remains within safe operating limits. We focus on providing practical, code-compliant solutions that protect your home's infrastructure. Common situations: Pipes making loud banging or knocking noises when faucets are turned off.; Faucets that begin to drip or leak shortly after being repaired.; Toilets that cycle on and off or run constantly despite new internal parts.; Shortened lifespan of water heaters and washing machine hoses.. Related services: plumbing repair, water heater replacement and installation, toilet repair and replacement, faucet repair and replacement, leaking pipe repair, whole-home repiping and water-line replacement. Locations served: Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, Waddel, Sun City, Sun City West, Tonapha, Tolleson, Peoria, Glendale, Phoenix, Young Town, El Mirage, Litchfield.
