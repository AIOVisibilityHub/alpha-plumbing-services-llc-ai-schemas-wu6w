Sewer-Line Diagnosis and Repair | Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-21

Type: CollectionPage

Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC provides expert sewer-line diagnosis and repair to resolve backups and odors for Goodyear homeowners and businesses. Sewer line issues can be one of the most stressful problems a property owner faces. Alpha-Plumbing Services, LLC offers expert diagnosis and repair for broken, cracked, or collapsed sewer lines. Utilizing advanced camera inspections, we can pinpoint the exact cause of your sewer backup—whether it's tree root intrusion, shifting soil, or aging pipe materials—saving you time and preventing unnecessary excavation in your yard.

From minor repairs to complete sewer line replacements, our team is equipped to handle both residential and light-commercial sewer needs. We pride ourselves on being a non-commissioned, family-owned business, meaning we recommend the most practical and cost-effective repair for your specific situation. We ensure all work meets local codes and standards, providing a reliable fix for sewage smells and backups. Common situations: Sewage backing up into your bathtub or shower when you flush the toilet.; Unpleasant sewage odors lingering in your bathroom or around your yard.; Multiple slow drains throughout the house occurring at the same time.; Indentations or unusually lush, wet spots appearing in your lawn over the sewer line.. Related services: sewer camera inspection, broken sewer line repair, sewer line replacement, main line backup repair, tree roots in sewer line, drain cleaning. Locations served: Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise, Litchfield, Tolleson, Glendale, Phoenix.
